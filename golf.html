I started playing golf when I was 4 and played competively up until the end of high school. Now, golf is definitely more of a hobby and I've grown to appreciate it in a new light. It's no longer a something to stress over, golf is just another area in life I get to better myself and refine. Secretly, it also allows me to shift my attention away from reality for a short while. New challenges arise every time I play on the course, every round is unpredictable. I don't think I'll ever get tired of it. At least I hope that day never comes. I also spent 12 years as a participant in the First Tee program. It's a nonprofit organization that teaches kids life skills-but it happens to be on the course. There are levels participants progress through like boy/girl scouts and I completed the Ace Certification in high school. When I'm home for summers, I am junior golf instructor with the First Tee. Teaching golf is another avenue of the game that I really enjoy. Guiding kids to accomplish their goals is an amazing feeling and spending time with them is always a blast.

Fun fact: I've made two hole in ones!
