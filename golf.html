I started playing golf when I was four and played competitively through the end of high school. Now, golf is more of a hobby, and I have grown to appreciate it in a new light. It is no longer something to stress over; it is an area of life where I can continue to improve and refine my skills.

Secretly, it also lets me shift my attention away from reality for a little while. New challenges arise every time I play, and every round is unpredictable. I do not think I will ever get tired of it—at least, I hope that day never comes.

I also spent 12 years as a participant in the First Tee program, a nonprofit organization that teaches children life skills through golf. Participants progress through different levels, similar to scouting programs, and I completed the Ace Certification in high school.

When I am home for the summer, I work as a junior golf instructor with First Tee. Teaching is another part of the game that I enjoy: guiding kids toward their goals is incredibly rewarding, and I always have a blast spending time with them.

Fun fact: I have made two hole-in-ones!
